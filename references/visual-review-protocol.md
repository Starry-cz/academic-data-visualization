# Final-size Visual and Scientific Review

Apply this protocol after deterministic output QA. Automated checks cannot decide whether a figure makes a defensible argument.

## 1. Claim and evidence

- State the single primary conclusion supported by the figure.
- Confirm that every prominent annotation is supported by the plotted source data.
- Define the observation unit, denominator, baseline, exclusions, and time window.
- Distinguish description, association, prediction, and causal claims.
- Confirm that the chart type answers the research question rather than merely matching a requested appearance.

## 2. Statistical integrity

- Show individual observations when aggregation would conceal a small sample or distribution.
- Define every error bar as SD, SE, CI, credible interval, or another named quantity.
- Record `n` and whether it refers to biological replicates, technical replicates, participants, folds, or events.
- Check transformations, zero handling, missingness, multiplicity correction, censoring, and train/test separation as applicable.
- Reject decorative significance marks that cannot be traced to a named comparison and test.

## 3. Visual hierarchy

- Verify that the most important evidence is found first without relying on saturated colour alone.
- Remove decorations, 3D perspective, shadows, and redundant legends that do not encode data.
- Use direct labels when they reduce lookup cost without covering evidence.
- For multi-panel figures, align panel labels and axes; use a hero panel only when the evidence hierarchy is genuinely asymmetric.
- Inspect the RGB figure and grayscale proof at the final delivery size.

## 4. Profile-specific review

### Journal print

- Set the final 89 mm or 183 mm width before evaluating type and marks.
- Keep text editable and embedded; rasterize only genuine image or data-heavy layers.
- Verify axes, tick marks, units, panel labels, caption definitions, and target-journal instructions.

### Keynote or product launch

- Review at 1920×1080 or 3840×2160 and at thumbnail size.
- Use one declarative takeaway and no more than three or four decision-relevant series.
- Preserve uncertainty, baselines, denominators, and source notes even when simplifying.

### Report or web

- Supply alt text and, for dense figures, an accessible data table or long description.
- Check text contrast and meaningful graphical objects against adjacent colours.

### Poster

- Review from the intended viewing distance and at full physical size.
- Do not reuse manuscript-sized typography.

## 5. Decision record

Record one of:

- `pass`: no must-fix issue remains;
- `revise`: list exact panels and changes;
- `reject`: the chart or data contract cannot support the intended claim.

Do not record `pass` merely because the file opened successfully.
